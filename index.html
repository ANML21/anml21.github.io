About ANML21

Engineering materials at the nanoscale.

Our laboratory develops functional nanohybrids, nanoporous materials and layered heterostructures using inorganic and carbon-based nanostructures. We investigate the connection between chemical bonding, electronic structure and material properties to guide the design of advanced energy materials.

Research

Our research areas

From fundamental understanding of nanoscale interfaces to practical energy and environmental applications.

01 / Research

Energy Storage

Electrode and interfacial materials for rechargeable batteries and other electrochemical energy storage systems.

02 / Research

Energy Conversion

Photocatalytic and electrocatalytic materials for sustainable chemical transformations and energy conversion.

03 / Research

Functional Nanomaterials

Two-dimensional nanosheets, hybrid materials, and structure–property relationships in functional nanostructures.
